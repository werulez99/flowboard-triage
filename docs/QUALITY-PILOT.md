## Concrete post-run correction and bounded profiling

A missing-context question explicitly naming its entry contract and requesting
`receive`/`fallback` previously returned state declarations without the locally
indexed receiving function. Acquisition now includes at most those two exact
same-file/same-contract definitions. It cannot choose an external implementation,
infer deployment identity, or attest that a self-call/transfer succeeds. Wrong
or absent contract names do not import a neighboring receiver. Existing source
and reading limits remain. A focused native-parser control failed before the
fix and passed afterward. Pure private diagnosis found the requested receiving
definition without changing the saved draft, paid input/response or ledger.
This newly acquired code has NOT been reviewed by another paid challenge.

Installed native inspection found a second concrete defect: an unpublished
review's private causal events could produce a missing-card hint, hiding its
actual review/budget status behind "Make room for the walkthrough". Card
availability now derives only from an exposed, publishable guide. Both compact
and expanded rendering also ignore stale capacity hints when no current guide
exists. The native blocked-state assertion now checks the actual selected-job
reason, not merely the existence of a preparation panel. No incomplete prose
was exposed in the observed failure; the misleading status was the defect.

Twelve bounded local `requestMetrics` samples on the four actual packets took
0.60-4.20 ms each and reproduced their input hashes. An independent source
index for local diagnosis took 11.405 s. Provider service and incomplete source
coverage dominate these observations; a speculative metrics/cache rewrite is
not justified. The prior warm-open 500 ms miss remains separate backlog.

An explicitly hypothetical 200-case workload using the three observed generation
times (mean 231.656 s) and the one challenge time (194.449 s), exactly two requests
each and no repairs, has an ideal service-work bound of 44.39 minutes at 32
sustained slots. Ideal concurrency would be at least 48 for 30 minutes or 72
for 20, before local work, quotas, backoff or dependent-path costs. Both exceed
the supported local maximum of 32. These few incomplete cases are not a
representative workload; actual model/account capacity is unknown. Increasing
allowance alone would not solve this. No capacity sweep or batch was bought.
